# Manual template

Use these sections in this order. Drop a section only if the interview genuinely produced nothing for it. Write in first person, in the tone the person chose. Every line should be something they said or confirmed.

```markdown
# <Name> — Operating Manual

*How my mind works, what derails it, and the rules I run on.*
*Version 1 · <date> · Review monthly. Update when a pattern changes.*

---

## How to use this doc
- **For me:** when to reread it (a broken day, before adopting a new system, before a big decision in a strong mood).
- **For AI:** paste as context; build and advise for this person.
- **What this is:** self-observation, not a diagnosis.
- **Tone:** <the tone they chose>.

## 1. Direction
Long-term goal. 1–3 priorities for the next ~3 months, in order. Which one wins when they compete.

## 2. The core engine
What reliably creates motivation and momentum. What counts as a win. Conditions where I work best and where I freeze. Whether external pressure or internal discipline actually works.

## 3. Recurring patterns
One short subsection per pattern: what happens, what sets it off, what breaks it.

## 4. Triggers
What sets off guilt, anxiety or comparison. Top distraction sources.

## 5. Energy and routine
Sleep, sharpest hours, crashes, how the night shapes the next day. Current routine as it really is.

## 6. Strengths — protect and use
What I'm reliably good at. Where my best effort should go.

## 7. Accepted limits — design around, don't fight
Traits that won't change with willpower. → Automate, delegate, or make them external.

## 8. Rules
Numbered, concrete, conditional: "When X → do Y." Each rule should trace back to a pattern, trigger or limit above. Typically 8–12.

## 9. Contradictions to watch
Where two of my answers pull against each other, and how the rules handle it. Include the risk of the manual itself becoming a novelty project, if relevant.

## 10. Signals it's working (monthly check)
2–5 checkbox items I'd actually notice.

## For AI reading this
4–6 bullets: how to build and advise for me, what to flag directly, what tone to use.
```
